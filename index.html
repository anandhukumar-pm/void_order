Payments & External Payment Flows

A focused overview of the key payment capabilities in the Mysa prototype.

1

Mark Bills as Paid

Mark a single or multiple bills as paid, capture payment details such as amount, date, UTR/reference and narration, and support partial or full payments.

2

Apply Advance & Debit Note in Cart

View available Advances or Debit Notes against a bill, select the amount to apply, and reduce the bill's outstanding amount.

3

Create an External Payment

Create a payment directly from the UI without selecting a bill. Capture the payment details and map the recorded payment to bills later.
